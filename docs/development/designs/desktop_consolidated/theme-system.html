AGNOS · Desktop Theme System

Six worlds, two temperaments.

Your divergent pass sketched six ways an agentic OS could look — and every one of them was quietly answering the same question: how do you make trust visible? This consolidates them into two initial themes, each in dark and light. Same OS, same trust architecture — one wears it loud, one wears it quiet.

neonbrutalistcassette ghostsolarpunkstillness → MUDRA SHANTA

Trust is the material. In one theme it is a seal you can watch snap shut; in the other it is the light a thing gives off when it means what it says. the through-line across all six worlds

I MUDRA मुद्रा — the seal Recommended default

Nothing runs until you approve, and the approval is a stamp you can see. Monospace, radius-0, the hash-chain on the surface. Verified glows cyan; unsigned glares red. It looks like a signed artifact because it is one — trust by exposure, not decoration.

Trust language

Every agent action is a public hash-chained row. Verified work carries the cyan ⬡ seal and glows; anything ▢ unsigned glares red until an audit agent countersigns. The seal is the only ornament — and it means something.

II SHANTA शान्त — stillness Focus mode

Productivity is not the sum of what is open, but the quiet of what is closed. The agents keep their work at the periphery until it earns the center. Thin type, warm ground, one living accent. Here trust needs no badge — verified work simply holds steady light; a single gold firefly settles on it.

Trust language

No badges. Verified work holds full, steady light; unverified work stays translucent and casts no firefly. Provenance is luminance — the glass itself testifies. The single gold mote is the only color allowed to move.

Two themes, one philosophy

They aren't rivals — they're the same sovereignty at two volumes. Ship both; let the desktop switch by intent.

◆ default · system-wide

MUDRA — when trust must be loud

The honest face of a sovereign OS: signed, small, hash-chained, radius-0. Best for the shell, the audit surfaces, the moment an agent asks to touch your files. This is what AGNOS looks like when it means business — and it's the one I'd set as the out-of-box theme.

◦ focus · per-app / on demand

SHANTA — when trust can be quiet

The same guarantees, dressed for deep work: warm, thin, one gold accent, everything closed but the task. Best for writing, listening, a single window at 6am. The seal is still there — it just stops shouting.
